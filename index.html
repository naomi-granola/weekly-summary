This week in 3

  1. The best efficiency week of the quarter. On 6% less conversion spend, cost per meeting fell to £72 (↓29%) and meetings rose to 4,201 (↑32%). The efficiency gap tightened to 62 clicks per meeting — into the green for the first time (was amber 76).
  2. Statics are carrying conversion. New-taxonomy archetype creative is ~48% of account spend. The US testing campaign books ~£70/mtg — roughly in line with the £72 account average — and the standouts clearing the framework gate this week are all Siha / creator statics (C61 £12, C64 £15, C65 £20 cost per account) plus two motion cuts.
  3. Partnership scaled to half the account — but the newest hooks book dear. Partnership creative jumped to ~50% of spend (was 43%) as the creator / Gravitas videos scaled. Busy Mind-ers now has the best CTR of the three archetypes yet the dearest meeting — cheap attention, low intent.
