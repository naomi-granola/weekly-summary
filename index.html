This week in 3

  1. Meetings got cheaper even as the CTR spike unwound. Cost per meeting fell to £69.82 (−6% WoW) and meetings rose to 4,073 (+8%), while CTR dropped back 0.94% → 0.74% and CPC climbed to £1.09. The efficiency gap tightened from 89 to 64 clicks per meeting — back in the green. The exact opposite of last week: fewer, more expensive clicks, but they converted.
  2. Spend rotated hard from partnership back to brand. C94 — last week's engine — was cut −85%, and partnership fell from 52% to 24% of top-60 spend. Brand now carries 76%: the legacy no-bots static concept06_3 roared back (+59%, £67/mtg) and a brand-new creator video, C122, became the single biggest spender at £28.3k with very cheap accounts (£19), though its meetings are still maturing.
  3. Archetype testing is still the efficient engine — and Busy Mind-ers flipped to cheapest. The three testing ad sets book at ~£45/mtg vs £70 account-wide; Busy Mind-ers £39 (dearest last week), Daily Doers £45, Decision-makers £57. Among live testers, the Newform "prep" partnership-video family (C129_g25) is the standout graduation candidate — last week's cheap winners (C93_g20_v4 etc.) have since been paused.
