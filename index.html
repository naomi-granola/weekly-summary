This week in 3

  1. Attention jumped, but the extra clicks didn't book. CTR nearly doubled (0.58% → 0.94%) and clicks rose to 336k, yet meetings fell ~10% to 3,785 and cost per meeting held flat at ~£74. The efficiency gap widened to 89 clicks per meeting (from 64) — cheaper clicks, lower intent.
  2. One creator video is carrying the account. C94 (Yassi Xiong, partnership video) alone took £37.3k — 13% of all spend — at 3.05% CTR and £79/mtg. It's behind most of the CTR spike and a big slice of volume. Strong asset, but heavy single-creative reliance.
  3. Archetype testing is the efficient engine — Decision-makers cheapest. The three testing ad sets book at ~£51/mtg vs £74 account-wide; Decision-makers £40, Daily Doers £52, Busy Mind-ers £61. Fresh Siha carousel and creator statics are converting well in testing.
