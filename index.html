This week in 3

  1. Meetings fell 5% on 9% more spend. ⭐£50.50 → £57.85 and 4,210 → 3,994 work-email meetings. Account→meeting held at 37.0%, so the loss is dearer accounts (£18.67 → £21.38), not worse conversion.
